# Human motivation and ethical psychology
Use Self-Determination Theory (autonomy, competence, relatedness), flow as a testable challenge/skill hypothesis, curiosity, mastery, expression, narrative meaning and belonging. MDA links mechanics → dynamics → aesthetics; verify expected emotion in play.
Map each design element to a player need and a possible adverse effect. Offer meaningful choices, clear feedback, voluntary goals, mastery-based progress and natural stopping points. Do not assume dopamine explains all motivation or infer addiction from session duration. Avoid manipulating vulnerability, loss aversion, guilt, fabricated near misses and artificial scarcity. No behavioral targeting for minors. Distinguish evidence from speculation. For wellness games never imply treatment or punish missed days.
Measure reported enjoyment, autonomy, confusion, accessibility and return intent with consent; use retention only as context. Compare qualitative feedback with aggregate metrics; never trade fairness for session length.
