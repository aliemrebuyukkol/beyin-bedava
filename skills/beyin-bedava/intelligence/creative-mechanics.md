# Creative mechanics, emotional direction and shareable moments
Generate three materially different mechanics from the Game DNA: combine two compatible verbs, invert a genre convention, or change the information available to the player. Specify input, state, feedback, win/fail, recovery, edge cases, feasibility and one risk for each. Compare against a simple baseline. Prototype the most uncertain mechanic, not the most elaborate one.
Use a beat map (anticipation → action → consequence → recovery) suited to the genre. Horror: readable controls, authored tension, safe pause, uncertainty without unfair input. Cozy: expression, gentle discovery, no forced urgency. Racing: skill and physical consistency. Narrative: choices with legible consequences. Learning: practice, feedback and recall.
Surprises should emerge from rules or authored discoveries, not manipulated rewards. Give players control over sharing; never post automatically. Make memorable moments through mastery, humor, beauty or surprising interactions. No fake rarity.

Score alternatives by changes to the player's decision and consequence. Record the rule, choice, feedback and risk for each prototype. Count independent approved alternatives and strategies that succeed in a playtest. See [experience criteria](emotional-experience.md).
