# Quality gates and playtesting
Record pass/fail/not-run/not-applicable with evidence for each gate: (1) build/compile, (2) deterministic unit tests, (3) gameplay integration, (4) actual playable route, (5) device/UI input/accessibility, (6) performance and memory, (7) save/migration/offline/interruption, (8) privacy and billing sandbox if applicable.
Use human playtests when available: novice, experienced, accessibility needs, returning player. Synthetic personas are scenario generators, not evidence of enjoyment. Measure completion, confusion, input errors, reported fun and qualitative observations. For experiments predefine hypotheses, baseline, sample size and uncertainty; never infer causality from a small sequence. Report negative results.
CI should fail on invalid schema, malformed data, policy gate violations and regressions; do not interpret missing data as safe. No production-ready claim with required gates blocked. Add reproducible commands and target device/build details.
